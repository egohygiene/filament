# Filament Manifesto

We want infrastructure that can be understood after the excitement of building it has passed.

We prefer reusable contracts over copied deployment folders, reviewable plans over opaque automation, explicit provider differences over magical portability claims, and consumer control over centralized lock-in.

Filament exists so infrastructure knowledge can become durable engineering material without turning every product repository into an infrastructure platform.

We will start small, prove boundaries with real consumers, document rejected abstractions, and only automate destructive actions when the safety model is clear.
